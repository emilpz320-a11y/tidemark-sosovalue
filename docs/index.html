How the agent works

  1. Collect SoSoValue API: spot-ETF flows, coin & SSI index snapshots, sector spotlight, news feed, macro calendar, FOMC odds.
  2. Score each asset on flows, momentum, sector breadth, news sentiment and market regime (each −1…+1, with the evidence kept).
  3. Decide BUY / SELL / HOLD with a confidence that rewards factor agreement and shrinks before macro events.
  4. Size with a 1% risk budget, volatility-based stops, position and gross-exposure caps.
  5. Price by walking the live SoDEX order book: spread, slippage, fill ratio. Thin books or high slippage block the ticket.
  6. Confirm: a human approves every ticket. Every run is replayed in the paper track record.
